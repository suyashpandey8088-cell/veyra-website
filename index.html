What’s included in an experience?+

Every listing clearly shows what’s included—typically expert hosts, required gear, permits, and select meals. Transport inclusions vary by experience.

Can I cancel or reschedule?+

Yes. Most experiences offer free rescheduling up to 72 hours before departure. Exact terms are visible before checkout.

Are the experiences beginner-friendly?+

Many are! Each card displays a difficulty level, and hosts share everything you need to prepare.

Can I book for a group?+

Absolutely. Group bookings are supported, and groups of 8 or more can request a custom private experience.
